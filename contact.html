Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about contacting us, starting a project and how we work with clients.

1. How can I contact AD Soft Technologies?

You can use the contact form on this page, email us at info@adsofttechnologies.com, or call +91-7012822057 (India) or +1 (917) 903-8066 (USA).

2. What are your working hours?

Our team is available Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 20:00. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next working day.

3. How quickly will I get a response?

Our representative will contact you soon after receiving your message. Calling us directly is the fastest way to get an answer for urgent requirements.

4. What should I include in my message?

Briefly describe your business, what you want to build or improve, any existing software you use, and your expected timeline. You don't need technical details, we can work those out together.

5. Do I need to know what technology I need?

No. You can start with the problem. Our team will evaluate your requirements and recommend whether an AI agent, chatbot, automation, integration or custom software is the right fit.

6. Where is your office located?

Our office is at 2nd Floor, Puthenpurackal (H), Puthenpurackal Lane, Judgimukku, Thrikkakkara, Ernakulam, Kerala. We also serve clients in the USA, including New York and New Jersey.

7. Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We have completed projects for clients in India and have experience working with clients in Europe and the USA. Meetings can be arranged online at a time that suits your time zone.

8. Can you improve my existing website or software?

Yes. We can add features, redesign interfaces, integrate APIs, connect AI capabilities and modernize outdated components without rebuilding everything from scratch.

9. What services do you offer?

AI agents, AI chatbots, AI automation, AI integration and consulting, website and mobile app development, custom software, software modernization, business automation, digital transformation and branding.

10. How do we start a project with you?

Send us your idea or requirement through the form. We'll discuss your goals, analyze the options, plan the solution and then move on to development, deployment and continuous improvement.
