Understand the Basics

What Is an AI Chatbot?

An AI chatbot is a software application that uses artificial intelligence to communicate with users through natural-language conversations.

Unlike traditional rule-based chatbots that rely mainly on predefined questions and responses, AI chatbots can understand different ways of asking the same question, maintain conversational context and generate responses based on the information available to them.

Depending on how the chatbot is designed, it can:

Answer customer questions
Provide information about products or services
Assist with lead generation
Search approved business knowledge
Help employees find internal information
Guide users through processes
Collect customer information
Connect with business systems
Escalate complex conversations to human teams

The capabilities of an AI chatbot depend on its underlying AI technology, knowledge sources, integrations, business rules and security requirements.

AI Chatbot vs Traditional Chatbot

Why Modern AI Chatbots Are Different
 Traditional Chatbot

Traditional chatbots generally follow predefined conversation paths. For example:

User: What are your working hours?
Traditional chatbot:
Choose an option:
Working hoursContact usServices
 AI Chatbot

An AI chatbot can understand natural-language variations:

"Are you open on Saturdays?"

or:

"Can I visit your office this weekend?"
...and respond based on the information available to it.

The key difference is that AI chatbots can provide more flexible natural-language interactions rather than depending entirely on exact keywords or fixed conversation paths.

However, traditional automation still has value for highly predictable processes. In some situations, a combination of AI conversation + predefined business workflows can provide the most reliable experience.

Human Handoff and Responsible AI

AI Should Know When Humans Need to Take Over

Not every customer question should be handled entirely by an AI chatbot.

For complex, sensitive or exceptional situations, the chatbot can be designed to escalate the conversation to an appropriate human team.

A well-designed chatbot should have clearly defined boundaries regarding:

What information it can access
What questions it can answer
What actions it can perform
What information requires human verification
When a conversation should be escalated
Which systems it is authorized to access

This approach helps businesses balance automation with human oversight.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is AI chatbot development?

AI chatbot development involves designing and building conversational software that uses artificial intelligence to understand user questions and provide relevant responses based on defined business requirements, knowledge sources and integrations.

2. What is the difference between an AI chatbot and a normal chatbot?

Traditional chatbots generally depend on predefined rules and conversation paths. AI chatbots can understand natural-language variations and generate or retrieve responses based on context and available information.

3. Can you build a custom AI chatbot for our business?

Yes. AD Soft Technologies develops custom AI chatbots based on your business goals, target users, knowledge sources, workflows and integration requirements.

4. Can an AI chatbot use our business documents?

Yes. Depending on the use case, an AI chatbot can be connected to approved documents, knowledge bases and other information sources so users can interact with that information conversationally.

5. Can an AI chatbot be integrated into our existing website?

Yes. AI chatbots can be integrated into existing websites through appropriate frontend and backend technologies and APIs. The implementation depends on your current website architecture and requirements.

6. Can an AI chatbot connect to our CRM or other software?

Potentially, yes. A chatbot can be integrated with CRM systems and other business software when suitable APIs or integration methods are available. Access should be controlled according to security and business requirements.

7. Can an AI chatbot generate leads?

Yes. An AI chatbot can engage website visitors, ask qualifying questions, collect relevant information and pass suitable leads to a sales team or connected CRM system.

8. Can customers talk to a human after using the AI chatbot?

Yes. Human handoff can be included when conversations involve complex, sensitive or unsupported requests. The chatbot can identify defined escalation conditions and direct the user to a human support channel.

9. How much does AI chatbot development cost?

The cost depends on factors such as chatbot complexity, knowledge sources, integrations, number of channels, user interface, security requirements and required AI capabilities. A simple website chatbot and an enterprise chatbot connected to multiple systems have very different development requirements.

10. How can we start an AI chatbot project?

Share your business objectives, current customer-support process, website or application and the type of questions you want the chatbot to handle. AD Soft Technologies can evaluate your requirements and recommend an appropriate chatbot architecture and development approach.
