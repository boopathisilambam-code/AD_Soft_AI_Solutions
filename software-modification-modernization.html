What Is Software Modification and Modernization?

Modification

Changing or improving an existing application to meet new business requirements. This may include adding features, fixing issues, updating interfaces, integrating APIs or improving existing workflows.

Modernization

A broader approach that improves the technology, architecture, performance, usability and maintainability of an application so it can support current and future business needs.

Businesses often need these services when software no longer meets operational expectations, becomes difficult to maintain or cannot integrate with newer technologies. We evaluate your system, understand your objectives and recommend a strategy based on your requirements, budget and long-term plans.
