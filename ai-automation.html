Understand the Basics

What Is AI Automation?

AI automation combines artificial intelligence with automated workflows to perform tasks that traditionally require repetitive human effort.

Traditional automation generally follows predefined rules:

If X happens → perform Y.

AI automation can introduce an additional layer of intelligence, allowing systems to interpret information, understand natural language, classify content, extract information and determine the appropriate next step within predefined boundaries.

For example, a traditional workflow might automatically send an acknowledgement whenever a new enquiry is received.

An AI-powered workflow could:

1Receive the enquiry.
2Understand what the customer is asking.
3Extract relevant information.
4Categorize the enquiry.
5Determine its priority.
6Add the information to a CRM.
7Notify the appropriate team.
8Generate an appropriate response.
9Escalate the enquiry when human intervention is required.

The exact level of automation depends on the business process, systems, data and controls involved.

AI Automation vs Traditional Automation

What Makes AI Automation Different?
Traditional automation

Traditional automation is highly effective when a process follows clear and predictable rules. For example:

New order received→send confirmation email→update order status
AI automation

AI automation becomes useful when part of the workflow involves understanding or interpreting information. For example:

Customer email received→understand request→identify issue→classify priority→retrieve relevant information→draft response→route to appropriate team

The two approaches are not competitors.

In many business processes, the best solution combines:

Traditional AutomationAIAPIsBusiness RulesHuman Oversight

AI Automation vs AI Agents

Which One Does Your Business Need?

AI automation and AI agents can overlap, but they solve somewhat different problems.

 AI Automation

AI Automation is generally useful when you have a defined workflow that needs intelligent processing.

For example:

New enquiry→classify→add to CRM→notify sales team
VS
 AI Agent

AI Agents can be useful when a system needs to interpret a goal, use tools and perform multiple steps to accomplish a task.

For example:

"Find suitable leads from today's enquiries, review their requirements, prioritize them and prepare the next recommended action."

Some advanced solutions can combine both.

If you're unsure which approach fits your business, our AI Adoption & Consulting service can help evaluate the use case.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is AI automation?

AI automation combines artificial intelligence with automated workflows to perform tasks that involve repetitive work, information processing, classification, decision support or other activities that can be handled within defined business rules.

2. What is the difference between AI automation and traditional automation?

Traditional automation generally follows predefined rules and workflows. AI automation can add capabilities such as natural-language understanding, classification, information extraction and intelligent processing to workflows where appropriate.

3. What business processes can be automated with AI?

Potential use cases include lead management, customer support, email processing, document processing, data entry, reporting, information retrieval, notifications and internal workflows.

4. Can AI automation integrate with our existing software?

Yes. AI automation can potentially connect with existing CRM, ERP, websites, mobile applications, databases and other business software through APIs and other integration methods.

5. Do we need AI for every automation?

No. AI isn't necessary for every workflow. Some processes are better handled using traditional automation, APIs or predefined business rules. We evaluate the workflow and recommend the appropriate approach.

6. Can AI automation process documents and emails?

Yes. Depending on the use case, AI can help classify documents or emails, extract relevant information, summarize content and trigger subsequent workflow actions.

7. Can AI automation update our CRM automatically?

Yes, where the CRM provides suitable integration capabilities. An automation workflow can potentially capture, process and transfer information into CRM records according to defined rules and permissions.

8. Can AI automation replace employees?

AI automation is generally used to reduce repetitive manual work and assist employees rather than automatically replace entire roles. Processes requiring judgment, approval or human interaction can retain human involvement.

9. How much does AI automation cost?

The cost depends on the complexity of the workflow, number of systems involved, AI requirements, integrations, data sources, security requirements and deployment environment. A simple workflow can be significantly different from a multi-system enterprise automation project.

10. How do we start an AI automation project?

Start by identifying a repetitive or time-consuming business process. Share the current workflow and systems involved with our team, and we can assess whether AI automation, traditional automation, an AI agent or another solution would be most appropriate.
