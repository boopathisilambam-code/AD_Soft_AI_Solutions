Our story

From a wrong number to forever

Four chapters, zero spoilers about who texted first. (It was her.)

2019 · Chapter one

Hey, wrong number

Karthik meant to text his cousin about cricket tickets. Ananya replied with a 14-point critique of his bowling. Neither of them stopped texting for three years.

2021 · Chapter two

The biriyani date

Their first real meal together. He ordered for two, she finished his half, and the waiter asked if they were celebrating something. They were.

A quiet evening, a very shaky speech, and a ring he had rehearsed with for six weeks. She said yes before he finished the sentence.

FAQ

Questions you were about to text us

Can I bring a plus one?

Yes, if their name is on your invitation. Use the guest counter in the RSVP and we will save them a seat and a plate.

Is there a dress code?

Each event has a colour suggestion. For the muhurtham, traditional silks are lovely. Anything you can sit and eat comfortably in is correct.

Will there be parking?

Yes, plenty. Valets will be at the gate, and Karthik personally promises there will be a space. He is, after all, a professional.

Can I take photos?

Please do. We have a photographer, but we want your angles too. During the muhurtham, please keep phones down until the ceremony ends.

What if I cry during the ceremony?

You will. There are tissues in every row, and Karthik has already cried twice this week.

Any rules for the dance floor?

One: there is no such thing as "I don't dance". There are only people who haven't heard the right song yet.
