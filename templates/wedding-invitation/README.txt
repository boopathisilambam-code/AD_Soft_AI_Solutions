Wedding Invitation - Karthik & Meenakshi
Open index.html in a browser (internet needed for Google Fonts and the map).

index.html        page content (names, dates, venue, map, footer links)
css/style.css     colours (:root variables at the top), layout, animations
js/script.js      countdown (date in variable T), gallery crops, RSVP, effects
assets/bg.mp4     hero background video
assets/photo.jpg  photo used in gate, story, gallery, journey, footer
assets/groom.jpg  (optional) groom photo - add this file to replace the illustration
assets/bride.jpg  (optional) bride photo - add this file to replace the illustration

Replace bg.mp4 and photo.jpg with your own files (keep the same names).
Photos in portrait (3:4) look best. Names and parents' names are in index.html under "Meet the couple".
