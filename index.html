AI Doesn't Have to Replace Your Existing Technology

Upgrade What You Already Have

Many businesses already have websites, mobile applications, CRM platforms, ERP systems or custom software. Replacing those systems completely may not always be necessary. AI can often be introduced as an additional capability.

For example, an existing website can be connected to an AI chatbot. A business application can be enhanced with an AI assistant. Internal documents can be connected to a knowledge-based assistant, and existing workflows can be connected with AI-powered automation.

Our approach is to first understand your existing technology and business processes, and then determine where AI integration, automation or software modernization makes the most sense — so you can adopt AI progressively instead of treating digital transformation as an all-or-nothing project.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers about our AI capabilities, software development approach, modernization services, and how we collaborate with global clients.

1. What does AD Soft Technologies do?

AD Soft Technologies is an AI-powered software development and business automation company in Kerala. We provide AI agent development, AI chatbot development, AI automation, AI integration, AI adoption consulting, website development, mobile app development, custom software development and software modernization services.

2. Is AD Soft Technologies an AI development company?

AI is now a major part of our technology offering. We develop custom AI agents, AI chatbots, AI automation solutions and AI integrations while also providing traditional software development services.

3. What AI solutions can you build for a business?

Depending on the business requirement, we can develop AI agents, AI chatbots, AI-powered assistants, automated workflows, knowledge-based AI systems, AI integrations and other custom AI solutions.

4. Can you integrate AI into our existing software?

Yes. AI capabilities can often be integrated into existing websites, mobile applications, CRM systems, ERP platforms and custom software. We first evaluate the existing technology and business process before recommending an appropriate integration approach.

5. What is the difference between an AI agent and an AI chatbot?

An AI chatbot is primarily designed to communicate with users through conversation. An AI agent can go further by using tools, accessing connected systems, performing tasks and executing defined workflows. The appropriate solution depends on the business use case.

6. Can you automate our existing business processes?

Yes. We can evaluate repetitive business processes and identify opportunities to automate them using software workflows, APIs, system integrations and AI technologies.

7. Do you develop websites and mobile applications?

Yes. We develop custom websites and Android/iOS mobile applications based on business requirements. AI capabilities can also be incorporated when they provide value to the application.

8. Can you improve our existing software instead of building a new system?

Yes. Software modification and modernization can include adding features, improving performance, redesigning interfaces, integrating APIs, connecting AI capabilities and modernizing outdated components.

9. Do you work with international clients?

Yes. AD Soft Technologies has completed projects for clients in India and has experience working with clients in Europe and the USA.

10. How can we start an AI or software development project?

You can contact AD Soft Technologies with your business idea, existing software requirement, automation challenge or AI use case. Our team can understand your objectives and help determine the appropriate technology and development approach.
