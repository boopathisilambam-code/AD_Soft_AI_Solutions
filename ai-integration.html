Understand the Basics

What Is AI Integration?

AI integration is the process of connecting artificial intelligence capabilities with an existing website, application, software system, workflow or business platform.

Instead of treating AI as a separate tool, integration makes AI part of the technology your business already uses.

The exact architecture depends on your existing technology, business requirements, data, security considerations and the AI capability you want to introduce.

For example, an existing website can be connected to an AI chatbot:

Website→AI Service→Business Knowledge→Response

A CRM can potentially be connected to an AI workflow:

Customer Data→AI Processing→Business Rule→CRM Action

A mobile application can include an AI assistant:

Mobile App→Backend→AI Service→Response→User

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is AI integration?

AI integration involves connecting artificial intelligence capabilities with an existing website, mobile application, custom software, business workflow, database, CRM, ERP or other technology system.

2. Can AI be integrated into existing software?

Yes. Depending on the existing architecture, AI can potentially be integrated through APIs, backend services, databases, workflow systems and other integration methods.

3. Do we need to rebuild our website to add AI?

Usually, not necessarily. An existing website can often be enhanced with AI functionality through frontend and backend integration. The exact approach depends on the website's technology and requirements.

4. Can you integrate AI into a mobile application?

Yes. AI capabilities can be integrated into Android and iOS applications through appropriate backend services, APIs and application components.

5. Can AI integrate with our CRM or ERP?

Potentially, yes. If the platform provides suitable APIs or integration mechanisms, AI functionality can be connected to selected CRM or ERP workflows. Access should be controlled according to business and security requirements.

6. Can AI use information from our database?

AI applications can potentially retrieve approved information from business databases through controlled backend services. Direct database access by an AI system is not always appropriate; the architecture should be designed around security and access requirements.

7. Can AI integration automate our existing workflows?

Yes. AI can be combined with APIs, business rules and workflow automation to interpret information and trigger selected actions within existing systems.

8. What types of AI can be integrated into software?

Depending on the use case, AI integration can include conversational AI, AI assistants, intelligent search, document processing, recommendations, content generation, classification, summarization and AI-powered automation.

9. How much does AI integration cost?

The cost depends on the existing technology, AI functionality, number of integrations, data requirements, security considerations and development complexity. A simple AI feature and a multi-system enterprise integration can have very different costs.

10. How do we start an AI integration project?

Share details about your existing website, application or business software and explain what you want AI to accomplish. AD Soft Technologies can assess your current technology and recommend an appropriate integration strategy.
