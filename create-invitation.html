Plan it right

When to Send & How to Word Your Invitation

Creating a digital invitation is only one part of event planning. Sending it at the right time and including the essential details can help guests prepare and respond.

Here are some general invitation timing and wording guidelines to consider when planning your event.

When to Send

8–12 weeksDestination or Formal Events

Give guests sufficient time to arrange travel, accommodation and leave, especially when the event is taking place in another city or country.

6–8 weeksGuests Travelling from Other Locations

Send invitations early when relatives and friends need to make travel arrangements to attend your event.

4–6 weeksMost Weddings and Major Events

This is a useful general timeframe for sharing full wedding invitations and other major event details.

2–3 weeksCasual Local Gatherings

For smaller and more informal celebrations, invitations can generally be shared closer to the event date.

10–14 daysBefore the RSVP Deadline

Give guests a clear response deadline and consider sending a friendly reminder before it closes.

Wording & Etiquette

Do

  • Clearly mention the event, names, date, time and venue.
  • Include useful details such as parking information, dress code and location links.
  • Provide a clear RSVP option and response deadline.
  • Use a warm and personal message that reflects your occasion.
  • Follow up politely with guests who have not responded.

Don’t

  • Make RSVP reminders sound demanding or pushy.
  • Send casual event invitations too far in advance without a reason.
  • Leave out important information such as the venue or event timings.
  • Assume every guest is comfortable using digital invitations; consider printed alternatives for those who prefer them.
  • Forget to check the invitation preview before sharing it.

Ways to Share

  • WhatsApp or Text Message: Copy your digital invitation link and share it directly with your contacts.
  • Email: Send the invitation link to friends, family members and other guests.
  • Social Media: Share your invitation link through suitable social platforms and private messages.
  • Event Page: Use your invitation as a single online destination for event details, photos, venue information and RSVP.

Everything to Know About Digital Invitations

Digital or Printed? Here Is How to Decide

A digital invitation is an online invitation that guests access through a link shared on WhatsApp, SMS, email or social media. Depending on its features, it can include event details, photographs, background music, venue maps and an RSVP form.

A printed invitation is a physical card delivered or handed to guests. Both options have their own advantages, and the right choice depends on your event, guests and preferences.

Digital Invitation

Best when convenience, quick sharing and easy updates are important.

  • Share invitations instantly through WhatsApp, email and other messaging platforms.
  • Reach guests in different cities and countries without physical delivery.
  • Update event dates, timings or venue details after publishing.
  • Include interactive features such as photos, music, maps and RSVP.
  • Avoid printing and distributing individual invitation copies.
  • Provide guests with a convenient mobile-friendly invitation experience.

Printed Invitation

Best when a physical keepsake or traditional invitation format is preferred.

  • Provides a physical card that guests can keep as a memento.
  • Suitable for guests who prefer traditional invitations.
  • Offers tactile paper, printing and finishing options.
  • Requires printing, handling and physical distribution.
  • Changes after printing may require producing new copies.
  • Guest responses usually need to be collected separately.

What Every Invitation Should Include

Whether you choose a digital or printed invitation, make sure it contains the essential event information.

  • Names of the couple, celebrant or event hosts.
  • Event date and starting time.
  • Venue name and complete location.
  • Reception or additional event details, if applicable.
  • RSVP instructions and response deadline.
  • Dress code or other special instructions.
  • Contact information for event-related queries.

Sharing It the Right Way

  1. Save the Date: Share the event date once it is confirmed so guests can plan ahead.
  2. Send the Full Invitation: Include all essential information, such as event timings, venue and other relevant details.
  3. Request RSVP: Ask guests to confirm their attendance by a specified date.
  4. Send a Friendly Reminder: Follow up with guests who have not responded before the RSVP deadline.
  5. Consider Every Guest: Keep an alternative invitation option available for guests who may prefer printed cards or personal communication.
