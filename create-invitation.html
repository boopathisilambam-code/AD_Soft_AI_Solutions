When to Send & How to Word Your Invitation

A digital invitation is quick to send, but timing and tone still matter. These are the rules of thumb wedding and event planners recommend.

When to send

8–12 weeksDestination or very formal events

Guests need time to book travel and leave.

6–8 weeksGuests travelling in

Relatives from another city or country, and shower guests.

4–6 weeksMost weddings & big events

The usual window for the full invitation.

2–3 weeksCasual local gatherings

Sending months early only gets it forgotten.

10–14 days beforeRSVP deadline

Then one friendly reminder about a week earlier.

Wording & etiquette

Do

  • Put the event, date, time and place in one clear line
  • Add helpful extras: parking, dress code, a map link
  • Say exactly how to RSVP and by when
  • Close with a warm, personal line in your own voice
  • Message or call guests who have not replied, and thank those who did

Don’t

  • Sound pushy in reminders – keep them short and kind
  • Send casual invitations months ahead
  • Assume digital is too casual for a formal event – a polished design and personal touches make it special
  • Forget guests who are not online – keep a printed card or a call for them

Ways to share

  • WhatsApp or text: one link, opens on any phone
  • Email: reaches everyone at once
  • Invitation link as your event page: date, venue map, RSVP and music in one place

With AD Soft you share a single link, so there is nothing to attach.

Timing and etiquette are adapted from public guides by Minted, The Knot, Joy and Sayi.do. They are general guidelines, so adjust them to your family’s customs.

Digital invitation guide

Everything to Know About Digital Invitations

Digital or printed? Here is how to decide

A digital invitation is a web link you share on WhatsApp, SMS or email. Guests open it on their phone to see the details, the venue map and the music, and reply with one tap.

Digital invitation

Best when time, budget or guest location is a concern

  • Costs far less, and the price barely changes with the number of guests
  • Delivered instantly, even to relatives abroad
  • Edit the date, time or venue later – the same link updates
  • Map, music, photos and RSVP are built in
  • No physical keepsake, and some elders may prefer paper

Printed invitation

Best for formal tone and guests who are not online

  • A keepsake guests can hold and keep
  • Works for every guest, whatever their comfort with technology
  • Printing takes weeks and cannot be changed afterwards
  • Cost rises with every extra copy, plus delivery
  • Replies are slower and harder to track
70–90%lower cost than print, by one wedding-planning platform’s estimate
80–90%of digital RSVPs arrive within two weeks, against 50–60% for paper cards
20–30%of guests still miss the deadline, so plan one friendly reminder

What every invitation should include

  • Names of the couple or the celebrant, and the hosts
  • Date and start time
  • Venue with a map link
  • Reception venue, if different
  • RSVP deadline and how to reply
  • Dress code or special notes

Sharing it the right way

  1. Save the date first. Send it as soon as the date is fixed, so guests can plan leave and travel.
  2. Send the full invitation early. Give more time to guests travelling from abroad or another city.
  3. Remind once. Send one RSVP reminder before the deadline.
  4. Keep paper for some guests. Elders who are not online may still prefer a printed card or a phone call.

Figures and guidance are adapted from public articles by Joy and Sayi.do. They are general estimates from wedding platforms and vary by event.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers about creating a digital wedding invitation, sharing it on WhatsApp and managing RSVPs with AD Soft Technologies.

1. What is a digital wedding invitation?

A digital wedding invitation is an online invitation you share as a link on WhatsApp, Instagram, email or SMS instead of printing cards. Ours open with an animated envelope and include your photos, countdown timer, event timeline, venue map, background music and an RSVP form.

2. How do I create an online wedding invitation with AD Soft?

Choose a template, fill in the couple names, date, venue, photos and music in the live editor, then click Publish. You get a short shareable link you can send to all your guests in minutes. No design or coding skills are needed.

3. Can I send the invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes. After publishing you can copy the link or tap the Share on WhatsApp button. The invitation opens instantly in the guest's mobile browser, with no app to install.

4. Are the invitations mobile friendly?

Every template is designed mobile first, because most guests open invitations from WhatsApp on their phone. They also look great on tablets and desktops, and you can check the phone and desktop view before publishing.

5. Can I add my own photos and music?

Yes. Upload your pre-wedding photos for the gallery and add your favourite song so it plays when the guest opens the envelope. You can reorder photos and add captions.

6. Can guests RSVP and can I see the responses?

Yes. Each invitation has an RSVP form where guests confirm attendance, number of guests and food preference. You can view the responses and download them as a CSV file.

7. Can I edit my invitation after publishing?

Yes. Open the editor again and click Update. Your link stays the same, so guests always see the latest date, venue or timings without you resending anything.

8. Do you support other events besides weddings?

Yes. Choose from wedding and birthday invitation designs, and edit the details in the live preview. More event categories are being added; contact us if you would like a custom design.

9. Can you create a custom invitation design for me?

Yes. AD Soft Technologies is a Kerala based software and design company. We can build a fully custom animated invitation with your theme, language and branding. Reach out through our contact page.

10. Does the invitation work in Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi and other languages?

Yes. Every text field is editable, so you can write your invitation in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, English or any language you prefer.
