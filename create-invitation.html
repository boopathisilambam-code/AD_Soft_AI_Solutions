Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers about creating a digital wedding invitation, sharing it on WhatsApp and managing RSVPs with AD Soft Technologies.

1. What is a digital wedding invitation?

A digital wedding invitation is an online invitation you share as a link on WhatsApp, Instagram, email or SMS instead of printing cards. Ours open with an animated envelope and include your photos, countdown timer, event timeline, venue map, background music and an RSVP form.

2. How do I create an online wedding invitation with AD Soft?

Choose a template, fill in the couple names, date, venue, photos and music in the live editor, then click Publish. You get a short shareable link you can send to all your guests in minutes. No design or coding skills are needed.

3. Can I send the invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes. After publishing you can copy the link or tap the Share on WhatsApp button. The invitation opens instantly in the guest's mobile browser, with no app to install.

4. Are the invitations mobile friendly?

Every template is designed mobile first, because most guests open invitations from WhatsApp on their phone. They also look great on tablets and desktops, and you can check the phone and desktop view before publishing.

5. Can I add my own photos and music?

Yes. Upload your pre-wedding photos for the gallery and add your favourite song so it plays when the guest opens the envelope. You can reorder photos and add captions.

6. Can guests RSVP and can I see the responses?

Yes. Each invitation has an RSVP form where guests confirm attendance, number of guests and food preference. You can view the responses and download them as a CSV file.

7. Can I edit my invitation after publishing?

Yes. Open the editor again and click Update. Your link stays the same, so guests always see the latest date, venue or timings without you resending anything.

8. Do you support other events besides weddings?

Yes. Choose from wedding and birthday invitation designs, and edit the details in the live preview. More event categories are being added; contact us if you would like a custom design.

9. Can you create a custom invitation design for me?

Yes. AD Soft Technologies is a Kerala based software and design company. We can build a fully custom animated invitation with your theme, language and branding. Reach out through our contact page.

10. Does the invitation work in Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi and other languages?

Yes. Every text field is editable, so you can write your invitation in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, English or any language you prefer.
