Understand the Basics

What Is an AI Agent?

An AI agent is a software system that can understand a goal or request, reason through a task, use available tools or information, and take actions within defined boundaries.

Unlike a basic chatbot that primarily responds to messages, an AI agent can be designed to perform tasks across connected systems. The exact capabilities depend on the business process, integrations, permissions and rules defined for the agent.

This makes AI agents particularly useful for businesses looking to combine conversational AI, automation and software integration.

An AI customer-service agent could:
  1. 1.Receive a customer's request.
  2. 2.Understand the intent.
  3. 3.Retrieve relevant information.
  4. 4.Check information from an integrated system.
  5. 5.Determine the next appropriate action.
  6. 6.Update a connected system when authorized.
  7. 7.Respond to the customer.

What Is the Difference?

AI Agent vs AI Chatbot

AI agents and AI chatbots are related but are not the same thing. Switch between them and see the same customer question handled both ways.

The appropriate solution depends on what your business actually needs. In some projects, an AI chatbot may be sufficient. In others, combining conversational AI with agent capabilities and business automation may create a more useful solution.

Is an AI Agent Right for Your Business?

An AI agent may be worth exploring if your business has:

  • Repetitive tasks that require human intervention
  • Large volumes of customer or employee enquiries
  • Multiple systems that users frequently need to interact with
  • Complex information-retrieval requirements
  • Repetitive lead qualification processes
  • Manual workflow steps
  • High volumes of documents or information
  • A need for intelligent assistance within existing software

However, not every problem requires an AI agent. Sometimes a traditional automation workflow, API integration, chatbot or custom software feature may be more appropriate.

Our role is to help identify the right technology for the problem, not simply recommend an AI agent because it is a newer technology.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is AI agent development?

AI agent development involves designing and building software agents that can understand requests, reason through defined tasks, access approved information, use connected tools and perform actions within specified boundaries.

2. What is the difference between an AI agent and an AI chatbot?

An AI chatbot primarily focuses on conversational interactions, while an AI agent can be designed to use tools, access systems and perform tasks. Some solutions can combine chatbot and agent capabilities.

3. What business tasks can an AI agent automate?

Depending on the system and permissions, AI agents can assist with customer support, lead qualification, information retrieval, document workflows, internal requests, scheduling, business processes and other repetitive tasks.

4. Can an AI agent connect to our existing software?

Yes. AI agents can potentially connect with existing software through APIs, databases and other integrations. The exact approach depends on your system architecture and security requirements.

5. Can you build a custom AI agent for our business?

Yes. AD Soft Technologies develops custom AI agents based on specific business processes, use cases, integrations and user requirements.

6. Can AI agents work with our CRM or ERP?

Potentially, yes. If your CRM or ERP provides appropriate APIs or integration capabilities, an AI agent can potentially interact with selected functions. Access should be limited according to business permissions and security requirements.

7. Can an AI agent use our company's documents and knowledge?

Yes. AI agents can potentially be connected to approved documents, knowledge bases and other information sources so they can retrieve relevant information. The implementation approach depends on the type, volume and structure of your data.

8. Do AI agents replace human employees?

AI agents are generally better viewed as tools that can assist employees and automate suitable tasks. Businesses should define which activities can be automated and which require human review, judgment or approval.

9. How much does AI agent development cost?

The cost depends on the complexity of the agent, required integrations, AI capabilities, data sources, user interface, security requirements and deployment environment. A simple informational agent and a multi-system business agent can have very different development requirements.

10. How do we start an AI agent development project?

Start by explaining the business problem or workflow you want to improve. AD Soft Technologies can analyze the requirement, determine whether an AI agent is appropriate and propose a suitable development and integration approach.
